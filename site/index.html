DAB+ radio,
made for the car.

DABplay+ plays straight from a cheap USB DAB+ dongle on your Android head unit. Big tiles, big text, few taps: made to be used while driving.

Features

Everything a car radio should do

Everything comes from the broadcast itself, so it works without internet. When there is a connection, it fetches station logos too.

Favourites at a glance

Eight big tiles on one screen, up to 24. Long-press and drag to reorder.

What's playing

"Artist – Title" from the broadcast, and the station's slideshow, shown whole.

Station logos

Downloaded by themselves when you're online, then kept. Offline, all else still works.

DAB+ and DAB

Both, with its own decoder for classic DAB (MP2) stations.

Follows your station

Signal gone? It finds the same station on another multiplex, and is back right after a tunnel.

Plays nicely in the car

Silent during calls, quieter under navigation, steering-wheel buttons, resumes the last station.

Traffic announcements

Optional: switches to traffic news and back, with a Skip button.

Your way

Day and night theme, four background tints or pure black at night, controls on the driver's side, player bar or side panel.

Screenshots

Easy to read at a glance

Designed on a 1280 × 720 head unit, and it scales to other screens.

Dongles

Which USB dongle?

DABplay+ talks to the dongle directly; no other app needed.

Tested

wRadio C100 / DTR-100

"DAB+ Receiver for Android Navigation", single antenna, sold under many brand names. USB ID 16c0:05dc.

Untested

Written, not tried yet

C100 Dual Antenna, Si4684 modules (Eonon A503, XFA-DAB220), Siano Rhine800 / Denver TDMB. Have one? Tell us how it goes: Settings → Diagnostics log makes a file to attach.

Not supported

Siano "Rio"

It needs vendor firmware that can't be distributed.

Install

Up and running in a minute

  1. Download

    Get the APK from the GitHub releases page.

  2. Install

    Open it on the head unit and allow installing from this source.

  3. Plug in and scan

    Allow DABplay+ to use the dongle. The first scan takes under a minute.
